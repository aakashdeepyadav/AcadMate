## Slide 4: The 5-Layer Anti-Proxy Protocol (I)
*   **Headline:** Layer 1-3: Hardware & Proximity.
*   **Layers:**
    1.  **Hardware Binding:** Account locked to a single device UID.
    2.  **Acoustic Proximity:** 18.5kHz near-ultrasonic verification.
    3.  **ML Face Liveness:** Anti-spoofing via blink/movement detection.
*   **Visual Representation:** 
    *   **The Shield Stack:** An isometric 3D diagram of three translucent glass layers stacked on top of each other.
    *   **Icons:** A padlock icon (Hardware), a soundwave icon (Acoustic), and a scanning face icon (ML).
    *   **Abstract Logic:** Each layer filters out "fraudulent data" until only the "Verified Student" remains.
